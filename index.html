HOW TO PLAY

  • Sun Chips pay for potatoes. They fall from the sky and SunSpuds make them. Click them!
  • Levels: 25 levels in 5 worlds. Before each level you see the zombies coming and pick up to 8 potatoes. Beating a level unlocks new potatoes and the next level.
  • Game Modes: Endless (pick your units, survive forever), Random Units (a random deck that always has a Sun maker) and Randomizer (no Sun: place free one-use units from a conveyor belt).
  • Lanes: zombies walk right-to-left; potatoes only shoot their own lane.
  • Coins: zombies sometimes drop coins. Spend them in the Shop on Rakes, starting Sun, resort armor and upgrade cards (Twin Sun, Gatling, Mega Bunker).
  • Vacation Mood: kills and safety raise a potato's mood; bites, resort hits and fallen friends lower it. Higher mood = faster and stronger!
  • Night World: no Sun Chips fall from the sky! Day spuds (☀ Surfer, Coconut, Ice) fall asleep, while night spuds (☾ Glow, Moon) wake up. Moon Spuds make your Sun Chips.
  • Controls: P pauses, the Speed button fast-forwards, S is the shovel.
  • Lose if the resort hits 0 HP. Win by surviving all waves. Every 5th level has a boss!
